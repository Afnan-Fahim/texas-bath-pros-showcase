# San Antonio Shower Showcase

Build a modern, professional, high-converting, fully responsive single-page marketing website for www.TexasBathPros.com — a local family-owned bathroom remodeling company based in San Antonio, Texas.



The website must feel trustworthy, clean, and premium — comparable in quality, layout flow, and user experience to https://legacybathandkitchen.com/ but focused exclusively on bathroom remodeling (especially tub-to-shower conversions and shower updates). Use a calming professional color palette: clean whites, soft warm grays, deep navy/teal accents (#0D3B66 or similar), and subtle warm wood or stone tones. High-quality sans-serif typography. Spacious, airy layout with excellent visual hierarchy and strong calls-to-action. Mobile-first and fast-loading.



**Company positioning & key messages (use throughout):**

- Proudly local, family-owned San Antonio bathroom remodeler

- Free no-obligation, no-hassle same-day estimates

- A+ BBB rating with ZERO customer complaints

- Specialize in fast, beautiful, leak-proof acrylic shower systems (inspired by premium panels like those from bellastonesystems.com — realistic tile patterns and stone/marble looks)

- Main purpose of the site: Let homeowners see real before-and-after transformations of finished showers and easily book their own free estimate



**Phone number (make clickable everywhere):** (210) 702-0753



**Logo:** Clean modern text logo "Texas Bath Pros" with a subtle, tasteful shower or water droplet icon in navy. Use it in the navbar and footer.



**Overall structure (single-page with smooth scroll navigation):**



1. **Sticky Navbar**

   - Left: Logo

   - Center: Nav links (Our Work, Why Us, The Process, Book Estimate)

   - Right: Phone number (large, clickable) + prominent "Book Free Estimate" button (opens form or scrolls to it)



2. **Hero Section** (full-width or strong visual impact)

   - Large, beautiful hero image or split layout showing a stunning modern finished acrylic shower (bright, clean, premium feel — white subway tile or elegant marble look with good lighting and modern fixtures)

   - Headline: "San Antonio's Trusted Bathroom Remodel Experts"

   - Subheadline: "Beautiful Showers. Fast, Clean Installs. Zero Hassle."

   - Trust line: "Family-Owned | Free Same-Day Estimates | A+ BBB Rated — Zero Complaints"

   - Two strong CTAs:

     - "See Our Transformations" (scrolls to gallery)

     - "Book Your Free Same-Day Estimate" (scrolls to booking form or opens modal form)

   - Small floating or prominent phone CTA on mobile



3. **Trust Bar** (immediately below hero)

   - Icons/badges row: 

     - A+ BBB Rating • Zero Complaints

     - Family Owned & Operated in San Antonio

     - Free Same-Day Estimates

     - Premium Acrylic Showers (Fast & Leak-Proof)

     - Local San Antonio Service



4. **About Us** (short & warm)

   - "We're a local San Antonio family business dedicated to transforming outdated bathrooms into beautiful, functional spaces our neighbors love. We believe in honest pricing, same-day estimates, and doing the job right the first time."



5. **Our Work / Transformations Gallery** (this is the most important section — make it prominent and visual)

   - Headline: "See the Difference — Real San Antonio Bathroom Transformations"

   - Sub: "Every project starts with a free same-day estimate. Here’s what we deliver."

   - Large, high-quality masonry or grid gallery with **before-and-after pairs** and standalone finished shower images.

   - Use photorealistic, professional images in the style of premium remodeling portfolios and bellastonesystems.com product photography:

     - Before images: outdated beige/pink tile tubs, old fiberglass inserts, cracked or moldy grout, dated 90s showers

     - After images: stunning finished showers featuring premium acrylic wall systems — realistic white subway tile with clean grout lines, elegant marble/veined stone looks (Carrara, Calcutta, Venatino style), modern niches, shelves, glass doors, excellent lighting

   - At least 6–8 strong examples. Include captions like “Tub-to-Shower Conversion • Alamo Heights” or “Full Shower Update • Stone Oak”.

   - Make images clickable to open a nice modal/lightbox with larger view (ideally side-by-side before/after comparison when possible).

   - Optional simple filters or tabs: All, Subway Tile, Marble & Stone Looks, Modern Minimal.

   - Strong caption or note: “All showers feature durable, leak-resistant acrylic panels installed quickly and cleanly.”



6. **Why Texas Bath Pros** (benefits section)

   - 4–5 clear benefit cards with icons:

     - Free Same-Day Estimates (no obligation, no pressure)

     - Premium Acrylic Shower Systems (beautiful tile & stone looks, lifetime durability)

     - Fast, Clean Installations (often completed in a day with minimal disruption)

     - Local Family-Owned Care (we treat your home like our own)

     - A+ BBB Rated with Zero Complaints (your trust matters)



7. **Simple 4-Step Process**

   - 1. Book Your Free Estimate (same day available)

   - 2. We Visit & Measure (no obligation)

   - 3. Choose Your Style (we guide you through beautiful acrylic options)

   - 4. Professional Installation & Enjoy (fast, clean, beautiful results)



8. **What Our Customers Say** (Testimonials)

   - 4–5 realistic, positive testimonials from San Antonio-area homeowners. Include star ratings. Focus on same-day estimates, clean/fast work, and how much they love their new showers.



9. **Book Your Free Same-Day Estimate** (the conversion section — make this excellent and prominent)

   - Large headline: "Ready to See Your New Shower? Schedule Your Free No-Obligation Estimate"

   - Subheadline: "Same-day appointments often available. We’ll call to confirm within 1 hour."

   - **Interactive booking form** (make it feel easy and professional):

     - Full Name (required)

     - Phone Number (required, pre-format nicely)

     - Email Address

     - Service Address / ZIP Code (San Antonio area)

     - What best describes your project? (dropdown or buttons: Tub-to-Shower Conversion, Full Shower Remodel, Shower Refresh/Update, Other)

     - Preferred Appointment Date (nice calendar/date picker component — show several available dates in the next 10–14 days)

     - Preferred Time (select: Morning 8am–12pm, Afternoon 12pm–5pm, or specific flexible)

     - Tell us a little about your current bathroom (textarea, optional but encouraged)

     - Big, clear submit button: “Request My Free Estimate”

   - On successful submission: Show a beautiful confirmation screen/modal saying “Thank you! Your request has been received. A member of our team will call you shortly to confirm your appointment slot.” Optionally show a summary of what they selected.

   - Reassurance text: “No pressure. No obligation. Same-day estimates available for most San Antonio homes.”



10. **FAQ** (optional but helpful — keep short)

    - Common questions about timeline, materials, cost range, one-day installs, etc.



11. **Footer**

    - Logo + short tagline

    - Phone number (clickable)

    - “Proudly serving San Antonio and surrounding areas”

    - BBB A+ badge / link (link to bbb.org profile if possible)

    - Copyright and simple legal links

    - Optional small note: “Quality acrylic shower systems featuring styles similar to Bella Stone Systems”



**Technical & UX requirements:**

- Fully responsive and beautiful on mobile (hamburger menu, touch-friendly form, large tap targets)

- Smooth scrolling between sections

- Subtle, tasteful animations (fade-ins, hover states on images/cards)

- All CTAs throughout the site should drive toward the booking form or phone

- Form should have proper validation and nice error/success states

- SEO-friendly: Proper page title “Texas Bath Pros | San Antonio Bathroom Remodeling | Free Same-Day Estimates”, good meta description focused on before/after showers and easy booking

- Make the gallery and booking form the hero experiences of the site



Prioritize visual proof (before/after gallery) and frictionless booking above everything else. The site should make homeowners feel confident, excited, and ready to book immediately.
